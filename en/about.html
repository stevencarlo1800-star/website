About Us

Your trust, our commitment.

Our Story

Founded in 2010, Alfahouse started as a small family business with a big vision: to offer reliable and accessible climate comfort solutions for everyone. Over the years, our commitment to quality and customer satisfaction has allowed us to grow into a leading player in the Belgian HVAC sector.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to ensure a comfortable and healthy indoor environment for our clients, whether at home or at work. We strive to achieve this by offering cutting-edge technology, unparalleled technical expertise, and exceptional customer service.

Our Values

Quality: We use only the best materials and equipment from the most reputable brands.
Integrity: We believe in honest communication and transparent pricing.
Innovation: We stay at the forefront of the latest technological advancements to bring you the most effective and energy-efficient solutions.
